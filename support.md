# Steady Support

Quick answers to common questions, plus a way to reach us if you need more help.

---

## Frequently asked questions

### Is my data really private?

Yes. Everything stays on your iPhone (and your Apple Watch and iCloud Backup if you've enabled it). We don't run a server. We don't see your data. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for the full breakdown.

### How do I connect Apple Health?

Settings tab, Apple Health, tap **Connect**, grant access to Sleep Analysis when iOS asks. Once connected, Steady pulls your sleep sessions from Health automatically when you open the app.

### My widget shows zeros / old data.

Open the Steady app once with your iPhone unlocked. The widget refreshes within a minute after that. If you just installed the widget, give it 30 seconds to populate.

### My Apple Watch complication isn't appearing.

Make sure (1) the Steady Watch app is installed (open the Watch app on iPhone, My Watch, Available Apps, install Steady), (2) you've opened the iPhone Steady app once after installing, (3) the watch face you're editing actually has a complication slot for the family Steady supports (Circular, Corner, Inline, Rectangular). Long-press the face, Edit, swipe to Complications, pick "Steady fatigue".

### How do I change my shift pattern?

Settings, Shift schedule, tap **Edit** in the toolbar (top right). You can adjust the existing pattern (start time, anchor date, phase, shift length, name) or switch to a completely different pattern. Your day-by-day overrides are preserved either way.

### How does the free trial work?

Steady includes a 14-day free trial of all Pro features. The trial starts automatically on first launch, no credit card required. If you don't subscribe by the end of the trial, the app falls back to the free tier and continues to work, you just lose access to the Pro features.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS Settings, tap your name at the top, Subscriptions, Steady, Cancel Subscription. Apple, not Steady, controls subscription billing, so cancellation lives in iOS Settings.

### How do I redeem a promo code?

Settings, **Redeem a code**, enter the code in the sheet that appears. You can also use the App Store's redemption URL we sent you.

### Will Steady work on iPad?

It runs but isn't designed for iPad layouts yet. Use it on iPhone for the best experience. iPad-optimized layouts are on the roadmap.

### Will Steady work without a paired Apple Watch?

Yes. The Apple Watch is optional. All core features (sleep log, fatigue score, schedule, coaching, Live Activity, home/lock-screen widgets) work on iPhone alone.

### Steady is showing me zeros for fatigue, what gives?

The fatigue score needs at least one logged sleep session in the last 7 days to compute. Log your last sleep manually (Sleep tab, plus button) or connect Apple Health to import existing data. Within a few seconds the home screen will populate.

### Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings, Export your data, choose where to send the CSV (email, files, share with another app). Includes your sleep sessions and shift override history.

### How do I delete all my data?

Just delete the Steady app from your iPhone. Because Steady doesn't have a backend, that's all it takes. Your HealthKit data lives in Apple's Health app and is unaffected.

### I'm having a problem not covered above.

Email us at **jcmappstudio@gmail.com**. We read every message and respond as a human (not a bot). Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a screenshot if applicable, it speeds us up by a lot.
